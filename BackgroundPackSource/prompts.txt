01_ruins_normal
Create ONE finished standalone wide 16:9 landscape background for BRODUN, a 2D side-view turn-based card battler. Original art direction combining Limbus Company's oppressive theatrical dark fantasy, Reverse:1999's elegant painterly atmospheric environment design, and Slay the Spire's clear readable battle staging. Hand-painted 2D illustrated game background, restrained brush texture, strong designed silhouettes, muted sophisticated colors, NOT pixel art, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D render. No characters, monsters, creature silhouettes, UI, lettering, logos, border, panels or collage. Horizontal continuous traversable floor, character feet baseline around 65% image height; unobstructed left quarter for hero and right third for enemy sprites, focal architecture in distant background not occupying these sprite positions. Bottom 25% subdued dark floor for card interface overlay, top 12% restrained detail for HUD. Rich but controlled detail in back layers with atmospheric depth. Output wide high resolution, preferably 1920x1080. Image must fill entire canvas.
SCENE: Abandoned moss-covered palace arcade inhabited by ruin gargoyles and moss slimes. Crumbling gothic stone arches, rain-wet limestone slabs, ivy and fern clusters at edges, broken abstract statues embedded in distant wall, tarnished brass torch brackets with a few amber flames. Dusty slate, olive green and warm old gold, diffuse overcast daylight entering through side arches. A small-scale dangerous traversed corridor rather than grand boss arena. Absolutely no intact statues that could read as enemy sprites in foreground.
Environment only: no monsters, UI or text.
